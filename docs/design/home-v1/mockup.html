A. Embedded

  • The launcher's top bar, research notice, live jobs and Quit stay in view while you work in a tool.
  • One browser tab; switch tools from the compact rail. Tools stay loaded when you go back to Home, so unsaved edits survive.
  • The launcher page stays open, so auto-close can't shut the app while you work in a tool.
  • Costs about 90 px of height and 60 px of width. Two sets of chrome: the launcher's across the top, the tool's down the side.
  • The tool keeps its own theme switch, so the two can differ until the tools accept a theme from the launcher.
  • "Open in new tab" stays available for side-by-side work.

B. Separate tab

  • Each tool gets the whole window and looks exactly as it does on its own.
  • Nothing to build beyond a link; the browser's tabs and back button work as people expect.
  • The research notice, jobs and Quit are only on the launcher's tab.
  • The launcher tab is hidden while you work, so auto-close needs a fix: count activity in a tool as use (small core change).
  • Orientation comes from tab titles and icons; each tool needs a distinct favicon and a title naming the study.

What to sign off

  • Orientation. The launcher's chrome is a dark band across the top with a light sidebar; opnca-r's is a dark rail down the left with a light top bar. Teal is the launcher accent; blue stays opnca-r's accent and the shared "info" tone.
  • Shared meanings. Status tones and their colours are copied from opnca-r (ok, warn, danger, info). State words stay as the launcher uses them today; opnca-r shows no job-state words of its own.
  • Navigation. Home, Jobs and Settings exist now. Analyses, Reports and Decks are shown greyed here only to preview the final layout; each appears when its workflow lands.
  • Theme. A top-bar button cycles System, Light, Dark. The choice is saved in the launcher's settings file, because the browser forgets it when the port changes each launch.
  • Type. System fonts, no vendored font. Table text 13px, body 15px.
  • Jobs. An activity panel on Home (live and latest jobs, message, elapsed time, a moving bar while running, Cancel) and the full list on the Jobs page.
